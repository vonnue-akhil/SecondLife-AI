# Sample photo credits

The three sample photos in this folder are real photographs by the people named below. Each one has been
changed for this app, as listed. The damage overlays shown in the app are drawn at runtime from
`server/src/data/sample-regions.json`; they are not part of the image files.

Changes made to all three: cropped so the device fills the frame, resized (longest side 1600 px),
re-encoded as baseline sRGB JPEG (quality 85), and all metadata removed (EXIF, GPS, XMP, ICC profile).
Nothing in the pictures was retouched.
